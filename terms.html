2. Description of Service

Quran 13 Line is available in two distinct editions produced from one shared codebase:

  • Quran 13 Line (Free): A free, ad-supported offline Quran reading application with an introductory 7-day ad-free trial and an optional one-time purchase to permanently remove ads.
  • Quran 13 Line Ad-Free (Paid): A paid, ad-free offline Quran reading application with no advertisements and no in-app purchases.

Both variants provide:

  • Complete 13-line IndoPak Quran text (847 pages) displayed as high-resolution page images
  • Optional downloadable Color Tajweed rules edition (850 pages)
  • 4-in-1 Reading Mode Sheet (Edition Switch, Persistent Zoom, Curl vs Scroll, Vertical/Horizontal)
  • Reading progress tracking (bookmarks, streaks, statistics, and Khatam history)
  • Comprehensive Islamic utilities: GPS Prayer Times, Kaaba Qibla Compass, Zakat Calculator with PDF export, Salawat Counter with SQLite analytics, 40+ Quran Duas, 99 Names of Allah, and Quran Quiz
  • Light and Dark theme options with Material 3 styling

Both variants are designed to operate fully offline after installation.

3. Privacy & Local Storage

The paid app (Quran 13 Line Ad-Free) collects no personal data whatsoever. All reading progress, bookmarks, settings, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device. No analytics, advertising, telemetry, or crash reporting is utilized.

The free app (Quran 13 Line) similarly stores all reading records locally on your device. However, the free version utilizes Google AdMob for advertising after the 7-day trial period, which may process non-personal advertising identifiers as detailed in our Privacy Policy. No ads are shown during the trial period, and ads can be permanently removed via in-app purchase.

4. Advertising

The free version of Quran 13 Line displays advertisements served by Google AdMob:

  • 7-day ad-free trial: No advertisements are shown for the first 7 days after installation.

  • After trial: Non-intrusive advertisements may appear in utility screens during normal usage.
  • Lifetime ad-free purchase: Users may make a one-time in-app purchase to permanently remove all advertisements.
  • Paid app: Quran 13 Line Ad-Free contains no advertisements at any time.

Advertisements are served by Google AdMob and are subject to Google's Advertising Policies. The developer does not control the specific content of individual commercial advertisements.

The Quran page images displayed in this App are digital reproductions of the classical 13-line IndoPak Quran edition sourced from public domain Islamic educational archives.

8. Islamic Tools Disclaimers

Prayer Times

Prayer times are calculated using the adhan calculation library based on your GPS coordinates and selected calculation method (e.g. Karachi, MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Umm Al-Qura). Times are approximate and may vary slightly from your local mosque. Always confirm with your local Islamic authority.

Zakat Calculator

The Zakat Calculator provides estimates based on user-entered asset values and standard Nisab thresholds (85g Gold / 595g Silver). It is a reference tool only. Users should consult a qualified Islamic scholar (Alim) regarding binding individual rulings, deductible liabilities, and debt settlement priority.

Qibla Compass

The Qibla direction is calculated mathematically from your device's GPS position to the Kaaba in Makkah. Compass accuracy depends entirely on your device's internal magnetic sensor calibration and may be affected by nearby magnetic or electronic interference.
