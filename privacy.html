Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026 • Entity: Dalcanciaa
Privacy Commitment: This Privacy Policy applies to the Dalcanciaa website (dalcanciaa.com) and applications published by Dalcanciaa, specifically Quran 13 Line (Free) and Quran 13 Line Ad-Free (Paid). We are dedicated to complete transparency, user privacy, and protecting the sacred Quran reading experience.

1. Quran 13 Line Ad-Free (Paid Application)

Package Name: com.dalcanciaa.quran.adfree

Quran 13 Line Ad-Free does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

This application collects no personal data whatsoever, including but not limited to:

  • No account creation, registration, or login
  • No email addresses or phone numbers
  • No precise or background location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No analytics, telemetry, or user behavior tracking
  • No crash reporting SDKs
  • No third-party advertisements or SDK tracking

This application operates fully offline and does not require internet access to function.

2. Quran 13 Line (Free Application)

Package Name: com.dalcanciaa.quran13line

The free version of Quran 13 Line stores all your reading data locally on your device. The app itself collects no personal accounts or contact details.

7-Day Ad-Free Trial & Google AdMob

The free application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob only after an introductory 7-day ad-free trial period. AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC.

Strict Sacred Space Rule: Advertisements are strictly forbidden inside the Quran reader screen at all times. Advertisements only appear modestly within utility and tool screens.

Google AdMob may collect and process the following data to serve and measure advertisements:

  • Advertising ID: Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings
  • Approximate Location: Derived from IP address for regional ad delivery
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information regarding ads served and user interactions

No ads are shown during the 7-day trial period. Users can permanently remove all advertisements forever by making a one-time in-app purchase.

For complete details on Google's data practices, review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-App Purchases via Google Play Billing

The free version provides an optional one-time in-app purchase (₹150) to permanently remove all advertising. This transaction is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Dalcanciaa never collects, processes, or stores credit cards, bank accounts, or financial data. All purchase information is governed by Google Play's Terms of Service.

3. Local Device Data (Both Applications)

The following personal reading records are stored exclusively on your physical device and are never transmitted to external servers without your direct action:

  • Reading progress (current Surah, Ayah, and last-read page)
  • Personal bookmarks and bookmark labels
  • Daily reading statistics, streaks, and Khatam completion logs
  • Salawat recitation session counts and active Dhikr progress
  • User application preferences (Light / Dark theme, reading modes, reminders)

You maintain absolute ownership and control over this data. You can delete all local data at any time via the "Reset" options in App Settings or by uninstalling the application.

4. Optional Google Drive Cloud Backup

Our applications provide an optional cloud backup feature allowing you to export and import your reading progress, bookmarks, and Salawat session records. If you voluntarily choose to use this feature:

  • Authentication is managed securely via Google Sign-In.
  • The backup file is saved directly into your own private Google Drive storage space.
  • Dalcanciaa servers never receive, store, or inspect your Google account credentials or backup archive.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Device Permissions

Our applications request only the minimum Android runtime permissions essential for core utility functionality:

  • Approximate / Precise Location: Required only for the Prayer Times engine and magnetic Qibla compass to calculate prayer schedules and Kaaba direction based on your GPS coordinates. Location is processed on-device and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Notifications: Used solely for user-scheduled local daily reading reminders and Friday Salawat notifications.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents the screen from dimming while reading the Quran Mushaf.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are designed for family and general audience recitation:

  • Paid App (Quran 13 Line Ad-Free): Does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
  • Free App (Quran 13 Line): Google AdMob is configured to comply with applicable children's privacy standards (including COPPA and GDPR-K). No personal data is gathered by the application itself from children or any other users.

7. Security & Policy Updates

We enforce rigorous organizational and technical safeguards. All cloud network interactions (Google Drive sync, Google Play Billing, AdMob) use standard encrypted HTTPS/TLS transport. Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be published directly on this page with a revised effective date and noted in store release documentation.
