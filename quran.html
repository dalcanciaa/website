⭐App Features

Everything You Need in One Quran Companion

Meticulously crafted features to enhance your daily Quran reading journey — 100% offline-first.

01

Authentic 13-Line Reading Experience & 4-in-1 Modes

Read the Quran exactly as traditional IndoPak printed Mushafs — 13 lines per page with ornate Surah headers, clear Ayah markers, and Juz boundaries. The redesigned 4-in-1 Reading Mode Sheet provides centralized control over edition switching, persistent zoom gestures, orientation, and realistic page curl.

  • Standard 13-Line IndoPak script (847 pages) in crystal-clear WebP
  • Optional 850-page downloadable Color Tajweed rules edition
  • Realistic page curl effect with authentic paper turn sound
  • Vertical continuous scroll or horizontal swipe navigation
  • Dual-page landscape mode for tablets with screen wake lock
  • Go-to-page dialog with boundary validation and Surah/Juz mapping
02

Glanceable Dashboard & Smart Navigation

Resume reading instantly with the Continue Reading card showing your current Surah, page, and progress. Access Surah Index with search, dynamic Friday Salawat counter banner, daily reading goal progress ring, and direct Tester Feedback to the Google Play Store.

  • Continue Reading card with last position and progress %
  • Dynamic Jumu'ah Mubarak Salawat recitation counter banner
  • Surah Index (114 Surahs) with Arabic/English search & Juz tabs
  • Daily reading page target indicator and streak tracker
  • Quick action tiles to Reader, Bookmarks, Surahs & Statistics
03

Quran Duas with Direct "Read in Quran" Navigation

Access 40+ authentic Quranic supplications categorized by spiritual occasion. Each Dua features Arabic calligraphy, English transliteration, and both English and Tamil (தமிழ்) translations. A single tap on "Read in Quran" jumps directly to the exact page in the Mushaf.

  • 40+ authentic Quran Duas with scholarly aligned page references
  • Trilingual display: Arabic, English, and Tamil translations
  • Direct "Read in Quran" jump resolving standard and Tajweed offsets
  • Favorite bookmarks and toggleable transliteration/translation controls
04

Salawat Dhikr Counter & Time-Window Analytics

Send blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ with dedicated session tracking. Features multiple Salawat options, responsive haptic feedback, Arabic-only mode toggles, and live time-window metrics (Today, Week, Month, All-Time) stored securely in SQLite.

  • Multiple Salawat options including Durood Ibrahim with count targets
  • Tactile haptic feedback and persistent session logging in SQLite
  • Time-window overview metrics: Today, 7-Day Week, 30-Day Month, All-Time
  • In-progress count persistence across app restarts
  • Friday Salawat reminders and real-time Friday home screen banner
05

Reading Streaks, Analytics & Khatam Tracking

Build a consistent daily reading habit. Monitor daily page counts against your goal, maintain consecutive reading streaks with celebratory confetti animations, track complete Khatam milestones with dates, and inspect chronological reading timelines.

  • Daily reading goal progress ring and pages remaining indicator
  • Consecutive streak counter with celebratory milestone confetti
  • Khatam (completion) trophy record with duration and personal notes
  • Ayah-level chronological reading history logs with date grouping
  • Optional cloud backup and restore via Google Drive (JSON archive)
06

Asma-ul-Husna — 99 Names of Allah

Reflect upon the 99 Beautiful Names of Allah. Each name features authentic Arabic calligraphy, English transliteration, comprehensive meanings, and native audio pronunciation with sequential "Play All" recitation mode.

  • Complete 99 Divine Names with Arabic script and English meanings
  • Audio pronunciation playback for individual names and sequential "Play All"
  • Flexible card-based contemplation mode and responsive 2-column grid view
  • Instant search and filter functionality
🕌Islamic Utilities

Complete Islamic Utility Suite

Essential tools for daily worship and Islamic obligations — all integrated and 100% offline.

🕐

GPS Prayer Times

Offline calculation engine powered by adhan. Five daily prayers + Sunrise, auto city detection, calculation methods (Karachi, MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Umm Al-Qura), and Android home widget.

🧭

Kaaba Qibla Compass

Real-time magnetic Qibla compass pointing directly towards the Kaaba in Makkah based on your GPS coordinates. Smooth needle animation with magnetic interference calibration.

💰

Zakat Calculator & PDF

Multi-currency wealth assessment with live gold/silver Nisab checks (85g gold / 595g silver), asset categories, 2.5% calculation, shareable PDF statement export, and debt jurisprudence guidance in English & Tamil.

📅

Islamic Hijri Calendar

Current Hijri date synchronized with Gregorian calendar, annual Islamic events catalog, Gregorian-Hijri converter, and 2-in-1 Month & Year Picker sheet with authentic Arabic calligraphy.

🎯

Interactive Quran Quiz

Engaging Islamic knowledge quiz supporting English and Tamil (தமிழ்). Multiple-choice questions, instant haptic applause, confetti celebrations, answer explanations, and report card modal.

☁️

Cloud & Local Backup

Local JSON archive creation and optional Google Drive backup sync. Safeguard your bookmarks, reading statistics, and Salawat sessions with complete encryption in transit.
