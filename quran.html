⭐App Features

Everything You Need in One Quran App

Meticulously crafted features to enhance your daily Quran reading journey.

01

Authentic 13-Line Reading Experience

Read the Quran exactly as traditional Indo-Pak printed Mushafs — 13 lines per page with ornate Surah headers, clear Ayah markers, and Juz/Para information. Switch between standard and Tajweed color-coded editions with a single tap.

  • Standard 13-Line Indo-Pak script
  • Tajweed color-coded edition
  • Surah headers with ornate Islamic art
  • 114 Surahs • 30 Juz / Paras
02

Elegant Home Screen & Smart Navigation

Resume reading instantly with the Continue Reading card showing your current Surah, page, and Ayah. Access Surah Index with search, Quran Duas, Bookmarks, and Reading History — all from one glanceable dashboard.

  • Continue Reading card with last position
  • Jumu'ah Mubarak Salawat banner
  • Surah Index with search & Juz/Para tabs
  • Reading Progress with streak tracking
03

Quran Duas & Salawat Collection

Access 40+ authentic Quran Duas with full Arabic text, English translations, and Surah references. A dedicated Salawat section includes Durood Ibrahim with recitation counter — perfect for Friday Salawat.

  • 40+ Quran Duas with Arabic & translation
  • Dedicated Salawat with Durood Ibrahim
  • Recitation counter for daily Dhikr
  • Copy, Share & Read in Quran shortcuts
04

Reading Statistics & Analytics

Track your Quran journey with detailed analytics — current progress percentage, pages read, Ayahs recited, weekly consistency calendar, reading streaks, and estimated completion timeline. Celebrate milestones with streak celebrations.

  • Current Quran progress with % completion
  • Weekly consistency calendar (M-S)
  • Pages read, Ayahs recited, streak days
  • Completed Qurans trophy counter
05

Reading History & Bookmarks

Your complete reading timeline with every session logged — Surah name, Ayah count, page number, and timestamp. Completed Qurans trophy section with duration records. Bookmark any page to quickly return later.

  • Complete reading history timeline
  • Completed Qurans trophy with details
  • Recitation log with Ayah count per session
  • Quick bookmarks with instant resume
06

Asma-ul-Husna — 99 Names of Allah

Explore all 99 Beautiful Names of Allah with Arabic calligraphy, transliteration, English meanings, and audio pronunciation. Beautiful card-based and grid layouts for learning and reflection.

  • All 99 Names with Arabic & meanings
  • Audio pronunciation for each name
  • Card and Grid view modes
  • Search and filter functionality
